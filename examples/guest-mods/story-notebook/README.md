# story-notebook

One small story quest in the story notebook, "The storyteller's lantern". When the mod is on, a
storyteller's letter waits in the Rito postbox (once Link has the Delivery Bag); the first letter from
a mod brings the notebook, and this one also a sealed letter for the storyteller's old friend, the man
at Windfall's harbour. Open the notebook from the Delivery Bag, choose the quest and "Show on chart",
and talk to the friend by day: he reads the letter and thanks Link with an orange rupee (100), and the
quest moves to the notebook's archive. The friend only says the quest's lines while this quest is the
one shown on the chart; otherwise he talks as in the game.

It shows `wwhd_quest_*`, `wwhd_quest_carry` and `wwhd_quest_talk` from
[docs/mod-sdk-v2.md](../../../docs/mod-sdk-v2.md#the-story-notebook). The texts are original to this example.
