# story-notebook

A small story mod that fills the story notebook. When it is on, its letter waits in the Rito
postbox; the first letter from a mod brings the notebook, which Link keeps in the Delivery Bag.
Once the letter is read, the quest "The storyteller's lantern" starts: open the notebook from the
Delivery Bag, choose the quest and "Show on chart", and Windfall Island carries the story ring on
the sea chart. A second quest is not yet available, a third is done as soon as the letter is read.

It shows `wwhd_quest_*` from [docs/mod-sdk-v2.md](../../../docs/mod-sdk-v2.md#the-story-notebook).
The texts are original to this example.
