# Original sky island assets

`assets/island.glb` (the floating island: its grass top with a clearing in the middle, the earth path and
ramp to the arena, the cliff round its rim and its rocky underside) and `assets/arena.glb` (the walled boss
arena north of it, paved in rings round a stone rosette) were generated by `assets/make_island.py` for this
example, MPL-2.0; the script reproduces them without external assets. Run it again after changing it.

The models carry texture coordinates and vertex colours but no textures: the game draws them with materials
of rooms of the player's own game files when the stage is built. The island uses Outset Island's ground, cliff
and rock materials (`"kit": {"stage": "sea", "room": 44}`), the arena those of the first room of the Tower of
the Gods' outside stage, under that stage's sky. Several of those textures are atlases (several surfaces in
bands of one picture); `make_island.py` keeps each surface inside its band, and its `ATLAS` table names the
bands. No game models, textures, collision files or archives are included.

The scene `bird` (the Helmaroc King carrying Link from Outset's pier to the island) uses the game's own
Helmaroc King and its flight animation from the player's game files; the package holds only its keyframes.

`assets/sky.wwht` is the stage's shader template, made with `tools/shaderprep.py stage-template` after playing
the stage: a code-free list of shader hashes and render settings (it passes the release guard's check). It
holds no shader code and nothing of the game's files; setup prepares the shaders from the player's own files.
